Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Lead Management POC ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information submitted through our lead forms may be collected and used.

Information We Collect

When you submit a lead form, we may collect your full name, email address, phone number, and other information that you voluntarily provide.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information provided through our lead forms to receive, manage, test, and respond to leads and to improve our lead-management system.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate and maintain our lead-management system.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information handled by the system. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Data Retention

Lead information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy or as required by applicable law.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact the administrator of Lead Management POC.
